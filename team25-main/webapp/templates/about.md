## About JMU Dining Navigator

**JMU Dining Navigator** is a web-based platform designed to help students, faculty, and staff easily explore and access dining options across James Madison University’s campus. With so many dining halls, cafés, and restaurants available, it can be difficult to know what’s open, what’s being served, and which locations accommodate specific dietary needs. JMU Dining Navigator brings all of this information into one centralized system, allowing users to quickly find nearby dining halls, view up-to-date menus, and filter meals based on dietary restrictions like vegan, gluten-free, or nut-free options.

Beyond simplifying meal planning, the system enhances the campus dining experience by offering real-time insights into dining hall hours and crowd levels, helping users avoid long lines and make informed choices about where to eat. By giving the JMU community a smarter, more efficient way to navigate dining options, the Dining Navigator promotes convenience, inclusivity, and better use of campus resources.
